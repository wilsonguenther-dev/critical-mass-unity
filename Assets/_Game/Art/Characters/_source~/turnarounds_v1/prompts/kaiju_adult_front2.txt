Paint the SAME creature as image 3 (same design, colours, crystals, proportions and painted style with bold dark outlines), but as a STRICT, SYMMETRICAL FRONT VIEW for a 3D modeller: the creature faces the viewer dead-on, its spine pointing straight away from the camera, the left and right halves mirror images of each other, both forelimbs equally visible at the same size, both hind legs equally visible, the head centred and facing straight at the viewer, only the tip of the tail peeking out to one side behind a leg. a NEUTRAL STANDING POSE for rigging: standing squarely on all four limbs, the four legs straight and spread apart so background shows between each limb and the body, weight even, the tail extended out behind and clearly visible, head level, MOUTH CLOSED with a small smug expression, calm.
- Design: the adult Kaiju: a heavy knuckle-walking quadruped with gorilla-like weight, shoulders about 1.5 times as wide as the hips, long thick forelimbs with huge boulder fists, short hind legs, the small head carried low between the shoulders, dark slate rock plates with glowing cracks, lime moss on the upward-facing plates, a tan segmented belly, a tall crystal crown and spine ridge running down to a crystal-tipped tail, yellow eyes, a small expressive face.
- NEUTRAL colouring (not aligned to either team): every crystal, crystal crown, spine ridge and glowing crack is ACID LIME / biomass green (like the egg's lime seams and the neutral hatchling), never cyan, teal, blue, red or violet.
- Flat, even, shadowless studio lighting; plain flat light grey background (#D9D9D9); no ground shadow, no text, no labels, no border, no other characters, no effects, no debris. Orthographic feel, no perspective distortion, camera at mid-body height. The whole creature in frame including the full tail, centred, with a small margin all round.
