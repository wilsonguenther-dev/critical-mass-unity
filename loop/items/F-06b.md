## Behaviour notes

- `Version` starts at -1 (`fill(-1)`), `Dist[k]` at `double.PositiveInfinity` (`fill(Infinity)`); `Cursor` advances with
  `(k + 1) % Count` (non-negative operands). The browser `Float64Array` version holds integers only, so `int[]` is exact.
- Fields rebuild on `WallVersion` change only (barricades bump `GridVersion`, not `WallVersion`, so they never trigger a
  rebuild: test P1). At most one field rebuilds per call to `MaintainFields` (round-robin `Cursor`).
- The miner Dijkstra is a separate heap from F-04's (as in the browser); port its tie order line for line.
