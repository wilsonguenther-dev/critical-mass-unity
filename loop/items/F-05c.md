## Behaviour notes

- `applyDamage` returns the damage dealt (0 when ignored); ported with the same early returns in the same order.
- Cleave walks `w.Hash` candidates through `SpatialHash.QueryCircle` in the hash's index order; the hit order and event
  order follow it exactly.
- Kaiju damage from a cleave calls `Kaiju.DamageKaiju` (F-05h); barricade damage calls `Barricades.DamageBarricadesInArc`
  (F-05e); wall breaks call `Abilities.MinerWallBreak` (F-05f).
