## Behaviour notes

- The route proof calls `FlowField.ComputeFlowField` (F-04). The memo test counts `FlowField.ComputeCalls` before and
  after its 1000 calls and asserts the difference is at most 2 (the vitest spies on `computeFlowField`).
- Placing and expiring writes cells through `MapGrid.SetCell` (F-02b), so `GridVersion` moves and `WallVersion` stays.
- A non-finite aim: `BarricadeTargetCell` uses `JsMath.FloorToInt` and the clamp; the result is `RefusedRange`.
